D-038 | 2026-10-27 | Capacity rule: a receiving facility absorbs extra Medicare stays per year only up to empty beds (certified beds − average residents per day) × 365 ÷ its own Medicare ALOS (PAC PUF; national median 27.1 days when missing), against the hospital's annual SNF discharges — entered, else 2024 Medicare discharges × 18.7% (MedPAC July 2026 Data Book Chart 8-3, FFS discharges to SNF, 2019) and labelled as an estimate. Allocation is greedy, largest sources first, spilling to the next-ranked facility with room; volume with no room anywhere is reported as "full". Empty beds are a proxy (they include non-Medicare beds and ignore seasonality); real admission capacity comes from the facility and replaces this when Rondo has it. | Matt: 'make sure we're not doing impossible things'. | Matt / Orchestrator | active
D-039 | 2026-10-27 | Optimize defaults to a Balanced objective: each SNF's national percentile (direction-aware, 1 = best) on QRP 004, QM 521, QRP 005 and Medicare payment per stay, averaged with equal weights (Custom blend: user weights 0–10 each, URL w=a,b,c,d); a measure CMS has not published drops out of that SNF's average rather than counting as zero.
