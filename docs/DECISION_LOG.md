D-023 | 2026-10-26 | Cohort builder: conditions are (field, operator, value) triples ANDed together; fields come only from the measure catalog plus five named identity/hospital fields; operators are an enum per field kind; numeric values are Number-checked, text values are escaped and stripped of ';' and control characters, star lists are limited to 1–5, hospital CCNs to 6 alphanumerics. D-032 | 2026-10-27 | Placement scenarios are a transparent reallocation: non-acceptable volume moves to the k nearest acceptable SNFs within a radius, split equally, and every outcome is a volume-weighted average of published CMS rates — no regression, no behavioral assumption, no invented elasticity. The acceptable-SNF definition is the user's (rating floor, flags, certification, units, overlap), the baseline is the hospital's real counts when loaded and the modeled service-area shares otherwise, and the page says which. Distance is between ZIP centroids (Census 2024 Gazetteer) and is from the home the patient would otherwise have used, until patient-home ZIPs arrive with real discharge data. | Matt: find the best-fit SNFs and model what volume shifts do to readmissions and cost; zero-hallucination rule forbids a fitted model we cannot verify on public data. | Orchestrator | active
