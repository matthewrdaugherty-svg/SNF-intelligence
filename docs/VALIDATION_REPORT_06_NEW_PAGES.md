# Validation Report 06 — Services layer, Guide, discharge mix / case-mix overlap, placement scenarios

Independent red-team check, 2026-10-27. Scope: the four features described in T-022–T-025 and D-029–D-033. Nothing under `app/src` or `scripts` was edited.

How the numbers were checked
- An in-memory DuckDB replica was built from the exact Arrow files the app loads (`app/public/data/*.arrow`), with the same `facility_master ⟕ snf_services` join and the same `snf_hospital_area` construction as `app/src/lib/db.ts`. The scenario SQL from `scenario.ts` was ported verbatim and run in that replica, AND a separate pure-Python implementation (haversine, nearest-k, equal split, volume-weighted means) was written from the T-025 description without looking at the SQL. The two were compared on 12 parameter sets.
- Rendered pages at `http://localhost:4173` were read with Playwright (build of 09:51, which includes the D-026 verdict fix in `Scenarios.tsx`).
- Guide claims were checked against 42 CFR 482.43, the 2019 discharge-planning final rule, the CMS Nursing Home Compare claims-based measure specifications, the Five-Star state cut-point tables (Aug 2026) and CMS MSPB-PAC material.
- DRG→family and MDC→chapter were checked against `docs/reference/msdrg_v42_1_list.txt` and the raw `hosp_drg` / `pacpuf_snf` tables; three hospital×SNF overlaps were recomputed by hand from those raw tables.

What checked out (so the fixes below are not read as "the engine is wrong")
- Johns Hopkins (210009) default scenario, independent Python recomputation: moved 0.55265 of volume, 99 receiving homes, 108 losing, unplaced 0.01787, 3.009 mi, QRP 004 11.664→11.578, QM 521 21.244→19.471, QRP 005 56.018→58.926, payment/stay $15,637.65→$13,981.45, 1–2★ share 0.3758→0.0117, average rating 3.178→4.480. Identical to the app's SQL to 1e-12 and to T-025 and the rendered page. Volume is conserved (before total = after total = 1.0) in every stress case (maxMiles=1, k=10, 5★ only, units nobody has, overlap 0.5 / 0.8, shift 0 / 0.5, 1-SNF rural hospitals 141316 and 051307, hospital 010051 with no service-area data). No double counting; a home that loses everything is dropped from the after set; unplaced volume keeps its original home's rates.
- Services: `svc=vent` in MD = 15, vent+dialysis nationwide = 86, Carroll Park (215085) census "90 residents · Medicare 9% · Medicaid 58% · other 33%" = 8/52/30 of 90 in `ltc671_raw`. T-023 counts (429 vent homes / 12,108 beds, 341 / 3,828 dialysis, 1,994 / 56,477 Alzheimer's, 591 hospital-based, certification-detail tallies) reproduce from `ltc671_raw`.
- Discharge mix: Hopkins "8,680 visible of 10,157 total" and "Cardiac surgery and procedures 912 · 10.5%" reproduce from `hosp_drg`; family shares sum to 1 per hospital; only one hospital in the file has visible > total (ratio 1.0019).
- Overlap: Hopkins×FutureCare Pineview 0.4685 (shown 47%), Hopkins×Future Care Northpoint 0.5785, Houston Methodist (450358)×Bayou Manor (676282) 0.4979 — all three hand-recomputed from `hosp_drg`+`msdrg`+`pacpuf_snf` and equal to `overlapSql` to 4 decimals. Pineview "published shares cover 91% of stays" and its chronic-condition percentages (dementia 47, HF 60, COPD 34, CKD 71, diabetes 74, stroke 51, depression 41; psychosis suppressed) match the raw PAC PUF row.
- Guide: SNF Part A rules (100 days / 3-day stay / days 21–100 copay / MA & ACO waivers), NF, swing beds, IRF (3 h/day), LTACH (>25-day average), hospice/assisted living, overall-rating construction, health-inspection cut points (≤10th percentile = 5★, state-relative), staffing-star inputs, abuse-icon rule, QRP 004 and 005 definitions are consistent with CMS sources.

## Findings

### 1. must-fix — "Homes that would receive patients" shows every receiving home as not rated on inspection, quality and staffing, and with no abuse icon
- Files: `app/src/lib/scenario.ts:88–89` (gains query selects only `f.overall_rating`), `app/src/pages/Scenarios.tsx:131–132` (renders `<QualityStrip row={g} />` and `<FlagCell row={g} />` from that row). Losses (`scenario.ts:90–91`, `Scenarios.tsx:138–139`) select `special_focus_status`/`abuse_icon` but still not the three sub-ratings.
- App shows (`/scenarios?h=210009`): Future Care Northpoint "5 – – –"; Autumn Lake Healthcare Post-Acute "3 – – –"; every row in both tables has "–" (tooltip "Health inspection: not rated") in the Inspect./Quality/Staffing chips.
- Data says: Future Care Northpoint 215147 = overall 5, inspection 4, quality 5, staffing 3. Autumn Lake 215330 = 3 / 2 / 5 / 4. Carroll Park 215085 (losses) = 2 / 2 / 4 / 2.
- Worse: with `flags=0&stars=3`, Complete Care at Severna Park (215143, abuse icon = Y) receives 0.57% of Hopkins volume and the Abuse-icon column shows "—" (no flag). The page is telling the user a flagged home is clean in the one table built to show where patients would be sent.
- Verified by reading `facility_master` in the replica and the rendered table text.

### 2. must-fix — Hospital with no service-area data renders "NaN%" and fabricated zeros
- File: `app/src/pages/Scenarios.tsx:109, 121`. `Number(out.before.total)` is `Number(null)` = 0 → `0/0`; `Number(out.before.low_share)` = 0; `Number(out.before.stars).toFixed(2)` = "0.00".
- App shows (`/scenarios?h=010051`, Greene County Hospital): "Of this hospital's SNF volume, NaN% goes to homes that do not meet the bar and could be moved to 0 acceptable homes within 10 miles..", card "Volume in 1–2★ homes 0% · before 0% · average rating 0.00 → 0.00", "rates cover 0% of volume", "SNFs in the baseline (0)".
- Data says: `snf_hospital_area` has no rows for 010051; every metric is NULL. The picker disables such hospitals, but the URL (and any link built elsewhere) reaches this state and nothing says "no baseline".

### 3. must-fix — The lead sentence misstates what the moved share is, and the unplaced share, whenever shift < 100% or nothing can move
- File: `app/src/pages/Scenarios.tsx:109`. `out.moved` = shift × (non-acceptable volume that found a destination); `out.unplaced` = shift × (non-acceptable volume with no destination). Both are multiplied by shift (`scenario.ts:70, 87`), but the sentence describes them as the share "that goes to homes that do not meet the bar" and the share that "could not be placed because no acceptable home is within reach".
- App shows / data says:
  - `h=210009&shift=50`: "28% goes to homes that do not meet the bar … 1% could not be placed". 57.05% of Hopkins' volume is in non-acceptable homes; 1.79% has no acceptable home within 10 mi. The 28% is the share moved, and the 1% is half of the truly unreachable share.
  - `h=210009&shift=0` (reachable by URL; slider min is 10): "0% goes to homes that do not meet the bar and could be moved to 99 acceptable homes" — 57% does; 0% is moved.
  - `h=051307` (Catalina Island, 1 SNF, no 4★ home within 10 mi): "0% goes to homes that do not meet the bar … 100% could not be placed". 100% is in a non-acceptable home.
  - `h=210009&units=vent,dialysis,trauma`: same contradiction (nViable = 6 nationwide, 100% unplaced, "0% goes to homes that do not meet the bar").
- Also the "unplaced" figure should not depend on shift: whether an acceptable home is within reach is a property of geography, not of how many patients are moved.

### 4. should-fix — Distance is NOT between ZIP centroids as the Methods page, T-025 and D-032 state; it is between Care Compare's geocoded facility points, and the numbers differ materially
- Files: `app/src/lib/scenario.ts:37, 65–68` uses `facility_master.latitude/longitude` (Care Compare `Latitude`/`Longitude`, 806 of which carry geocoding footnote 22 = ZIP-level fallback). `app/src/pages/About.tsx:79` says "straight-line distance between ZIP-code centroids from the Census 2024 Gazetteer"; `docs/TRUTH_LEDGER.md:156` "haversine between ZIP centroids, T-024"; `docs/DECISION_LOG.md:36` "Distance is between ZIP centroids (Census 2024 Gazetteer)". `zip_centroids.arrow` (778 KB) is loaded by `db.ts` and never queried by any page.
- Data says: 0 of 14,690 facility points equal their ZIP centroid; mean distance between a facility's point and its ZIP centroid is 2.49 mi; 84 facilities have no ZCTA centroid at all. Re-running the Hopkins default with ZIP centroids substituted gives 93 receiving homes (not 99), unplaced 1.97% (not 1.79%), 3.12 mi, QRP 004 after 11.70% (not 11.58%), payment/stay $14,230 (not $13,981).
- The code's method is arguably the better one (and avoids the 84 null-centroid homes), but the stated method must match the code.

### 5. should-fix — "Moved patients would travel on average X miles farther than the home they would otherwise have used" overstates what is measured
- File: `app/src/pages/Scenarios.tsx:113`; same wording in T-025 ("+3.0 miles average").
- What is computed (`scenario.ts:86`): the volume-weighted straight-line distance between the losing SNF and the receiving SNF. That is an upper bound on the extra travel from the patient's home (triangle inequality); the receiving home can be nearer the patient than the original one. "travel … farther" asserts an increase that the model cannot know. The control help text ("From the SNF they would otherwise have used") is honest; the result sentence is not. Suggest "would be, on average, X miles from the home they would otherwise have used".

### 6. should-fix — Reported-baseline path: an uploaded SNF CCN that is not in Care Compare is counted as "could not be placed because no acceptable home is within reach" and inflates the moved share

### 7. should-fix — Kidney-transplant DRGs are filed under "Kidney failure and dialysis-related" and hip-fracture hip replacements under "Joint replacement"; two remainder families repeat the name of a family carved out of them
- File: `scripts/11_load_drg.py:41` lists 650, 651, 652 and 019 in "Kidney failure and dialysis-related"; `:44` lists 008 in "Transplant". Reference list: 650/651 = "Kidney Transplant with Hemodialysis with/without MCC", 652 = "Kidney Transplant", 019 = "Simultaneous Pancreas and Kidney Transplant with Hemodialysis", 008 = the same procedure without hemodialysis.
  - Hopkins page shows Transplant 41 and Kidney failure 258; 73 of the 258 are kidney transplants (650: 20, 652: 53), so transplants are really 114. Nationally 8,819 kidney-transplant discharges are labelled kidney failure.
- `:29` puts 521/522 "Hip Replacement with Principal Diagnosis of Hip Fracture" in "Joint replacement (hip, knee)" rather than "Hip and femur fracture or surgery": 44,806 discharges nationally, i.e. 30% of what a discharge planner would call hip-fracture volume (102,368 in the fracture family) is shown as elective-style joint replacement.
- `:26` names the MDC 18 remainder "Sepsis and infections" although every sepsis DRG (871/872; 870 is in the ventilator family) has been removed from it (nationally "Sepsis" 681,117 vs "Sepsis and infections" 107,426 sit side by side on the page); likewise the MDC 01 remainder is "Stroke, brain and nervous system" after all stroke DRGs 061–069 went to "Stroke". A reader sees two sepsis rows and two stroke rows.
- Note also `:57`: DRGs 981–989 ("O.R. procedures unrelated to principal diagnosis") are assigned to the *aftercare* chapter `hlthsrv` for the overlap; 0.44% of national discharges, no clinical basis, but it only moves overlap by a fraction of a point.

### 8. should-fix — The MDC→ICD-chapter crosswalk puts hospital fracture discharges in the wrong chapter, and the two sides of the "case-mix overlap" are coded at different stages, which the page does not say
- File: `scripts/11_load_drg.py:53–55` maps MDC 08 wholesale to `sknmussystm`. MDC 08 contains the fracture DRGs (480–482 hip & femur procedures, 533–536 fractures of femur / hip & pelvis, 521–522, 542–566 other fractures), whose principal ICD-10 diagnoses are S-codes = chapter XIX, which the PAC PUF counts in `injpois`. So a hospital's hip-fracture patients are matched against the SNF's arthritis/aftercare bin instead of its fracture bin.
- Magnitude: SNF side (homes with ≥60% published) averages `injpois` 20.0%, `hlthsrv` 12.1%, `infctn` 4.7%; hospital side averages `injpois` 1.2%, `hlthsrv` 1.4%, `infctn` 25.2%. Fractures are 4.9% of national visible discharges. Re-mapping fracture DRGs to `injpois` moves Hopkins×Pineview from 46.9% to 48.2% and Houston Methodist×Bayou Manor from 49.8% to 51.2%; for a community hospital with a large fracture share the shift is larger.
- The second, structural point: the hospital chapter is the acute principal diagnosis (sepsis 25%) while the SNF chapter is the SNF-stay primary diagnosis (aftercare Z-codes 12%, the sequela rather than the sepsis), so the "same footing" claim in D-031 / T-024 is not right and overlaps are capped well below 1 for every pair (Hopkins' service area: mean 0.51, max 0.73). The Scenarios slider offers thresholds up to 80% that no home can meet (`ov=80` → "0 homes nationwide meet the bar"). The method caption should say that the maximum attainable overlap is structurally limited and why.

### 9. should-fix — Guide: three definitions stated more strongly or differently than CMS
- `app/src/pages/Guide.tsx:28` (QM 521): "the share sent back to a hospital within their short stay". CMS spec: readmitted for an unplanned inpatient or observation stay within 30 days of entry to the nursing home, counted "regardless of whether they were discharged from the nursing home prior to the hospital readmission" (Nursing Home Compare Claims-Based Measures Technical Specifications, 2019). The window is 30 days from admission, not the length of the stay, and includes post-discharge readmissions.
- `Guide.tsx:17`: "by law the hospital must honor patient choice and may rank options but not steer". 42 CFR 482.43(d) says the hospital "must not specify or otherwise limit the qualified providers or suppliers that are available to the patient" and (a)(8) must assist by "using and sharing data … on quality measures and data on resource use measures"; the 2019 final rule says to present objective data "on all available PAC providers" and warns that purposeful steering is non-compliance. Neither says a hospital "may rank". Drop "may rank options" or soften to "may share quality data".
- `Guide.tsx:10` (Distinct part): "A certified SNF unit inside a larger facility, often a hospital … surveyed and rated as its own provider with its own CCN". In this data 566 of the 577 "SNF/NF Distinct Part" homes are freestanding nursing homes (hospital_based = No); for them the distinct part is the Medicare-certified portion of the same nursing home, under the one CCN and rating the page already shows. The sentence is true only of the 11 hospital-based ones.
- Minor: `Guide.tsx:31` says MSPB (QRP 006) is "compared with the national average (1.00)"; CMS reports it relative to the national *median* provider. `Guide.tsx:36` names a commercial product ("Rondo") as the source of admissions criteria — unverifiable and out of place in a definitions page.

## Verdict

The arithmetic of all four features is sound where it was recomputed: the Hopkins scenario, its eleven stress variants, three hand-computed overlaps, the services counts and the Pineview patient mix all reproduce exactly from the raw tables, and volume is conserved in every case. The problems are in what the pages *say* about those numbers. Three must be fixed before anyone acts on a scenario: the receiving/losing tables print "not rated" and "no abuse icon" for homes that are rated and flagged (finding 1); a hospital with no baseline prints "NaN%" and invented zeros (2); and the headline sentence describes the moved share as the non-acceptable share and halves the unreachable share whenever the shift slider is below 100% (3). The distance method is misdescribed in the Methods page, the ledger and the decision log — the code uses facility coordinates, not ZIP centroids, and the two give different answers (4) — and "miles farther" claims an increase the model cannot measure (5). The discharge-family and chapter mappings are mechanically correct against the reference list but mislabel kidney transplants, hip-fracture replacements and the sepsis/stroke remainders, and the overlap's two sides are coded at different clinical stages, which caps every overlap well below the slider's range (7–8). The Guide is accurate on program rules with three exceptions worth fixing: the QM 521 window, the "may rank options" claim and the distinct-part description (9). The e2e test pins rendered strings and does not replicate the arithmetic as T-025 implies (11).
